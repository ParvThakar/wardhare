System Topology & Wiring

Connections & Bus Pathways

Understand the physical signal and power routing inside your rig. From raw AC conversion to high-speed PCIe 5.0 lane topology and front panel header pinouts.

🔌

24-Pin ATX Main Power

Feeds the motherboard chipset, onboard logic, PCIe mechanical slot power (up to 75W), and DDR5 DIMM subchannels.

Locking Clip: Ensure the plastic retention clip fully engages with an audible latch.
  • Carries +12V, +5V, +3.3V, and -12V rails
  • Includes PS_ON# green wire for soft power trigger
⚡

8-Pin EPS CPU 12V Power

Located at the top-left edge of the motherboard. Dedicated exclusively to supplying high-current 12V DC to the CPU VRMs.

Keying Warning: Do not force an 8-Pin PCIe GPU cable into the EPS header—the square/rounded pin keying is opposite!
  • Can split into 4+4 pin for older 4-pin motherboards
  • Enthusiast motherboards provide two 8-pin sockets for overclocking
🎮

PCIe 6+2 & 12V-2x6 (16-Pin)

High-power auxiliary feeds directly into discrete GPUs for rendering and machine learning workloads.

Critical Flush Seating: 12VHPWR / 12V-2x6 cables must be pushed in with ZERO gap between connector shells to prevent thermal issues.
  • Standard 8-Pin PCIe supplies up to 150W
  • 12V-2x6 delivers up to 600W through 4 sideband sense pins
